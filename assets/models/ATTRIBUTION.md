## Runtime detail pass
Original GLBs remain unchanged. model-finish.js adds original concentric optical geometry to the three rear phone cameras and adjusts physical material properties (coated optics, frosted back, polished frame). MacBook keeps its geometry/maps; aluminum, key legends and screen finish are adjusted. This does not change the source licenses or resolve the unconfirmed phone rights noted above.
